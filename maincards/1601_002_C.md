---
title: "Immediate Contact with Reality Is Impossible"
---

4th Century Indian philosopher Vasabandhu argued that reality is mental only.
Matter is just a convention we all agree on.
George Berkeley (1685-1753) argued that reality is only mental because the mind cannot make direct contact with matter.
The mind can only interact with its own thoughts, ideas, and perception.
Immediate contact with reality is impossible.

Westerhoff, J. *Reality*
