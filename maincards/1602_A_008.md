---
title: "Tracking Theory"
id: "1602_A_008"
---

Knowledge is having the right answer AND if things were different you'd still have the right answer.
Ex:
If asked if my dog is white, I answer yes, but if my dog somehow wasn't, I'd answer no.

S knows that p if and only if p is true; S believes that p; if p were not true, S would not believe that p.

Proposed by Robert Nozick.
