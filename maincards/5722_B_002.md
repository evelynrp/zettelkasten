id: "5722_B_002"

Effective librarianship needs to serve all, e.g. rural populations without access to broadband, cellular deadzones, and lack the resources and/or support to relocate.
This requires grassroots organizing.
"Full access to information by all community members is too large a job and requires the input of too many people for advocacy by the few to the few to be effective. Advocacy does not build local capacity or transformative agency among community members."
