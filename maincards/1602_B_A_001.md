---
title: "Academic Scepticism"
id: "1602_B_A_001:
---

Originated within Plato's Academy, as a response to Stoicism.
For Stoics, knowledge is wise judgement of right impressions.
Academic Sceptics argue that right impressions are impossible.
Human perception is limited, after all, so knowledge is not possible.

Key thinkers: Arcesilaus (3rd Century BCE), Carneades (2nd Century BCE)
