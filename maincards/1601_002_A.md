---
title: "Intersubjective Agreement"
---

We could say that reality is what we see, hear, touch, taste.
What if you are delusional?
We can say it is what most people perceive most of the time.
What about mass hysteria or Koro outbreaks (a disease that makes you anxious your dick will retreat into your body and kill you which is epidemic and often results in genital self-mutilation)?
It seems reality must depend on more than intersubjective agreement.

Westerhoff, J. *Reality*
