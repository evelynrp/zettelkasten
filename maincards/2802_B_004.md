---
title: "World Society"
id: "2802_B_004"
---

There is only one society, and it is global. Functional systems - economy, science, law - operate across the entire globe, not within national borders. Nation-states are organizations within world society, not separate societies. World society is radically asymmetrical: global communications coexist with deeply unequal local conditions of inclusion and exclusion.
