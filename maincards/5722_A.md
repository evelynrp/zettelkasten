---
title: "Measurable Goals"
id: "5722_A"
---

Because librarians often have hoarder tendencies, they need to be guided by measurable goals in creating rich information ecosystems.
Ex: public library working directly with K-12 school districts; academic library matching collection to degree programs.
Programs and services should address actual needs for actual members of the community.
