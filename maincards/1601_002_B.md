---
title: "'I refute it thus.'"
---

> After we came out of the church, we stood talking for some time together of Bishop Berkeley's ingenious sophistry to prove the nonexistence of matter, and that everything in the universe is merely ideal.
> I observed, that though we are satisfied his doctrine is not true, it is impossible to refute it.
> I never shall forget the alacritty with which Johnson answered, striking his foot with mighty force against a large stone, till he rebounded from it--"I refute it thus."

Samuel Johnson, lexicographer
