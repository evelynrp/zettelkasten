---
title: "Zhuangzi's Butterfly Dream"
id: "1601_002"
---

> Last night, myself, Zhuangzi, dreamt he was a butterfly, a butterfly flying about, feeling that it was enjoying itself.
> I did not know that it was Zhuangzi.
> Suddenly I awoke, and was myself again, the veritable Zhuangzi.
> I did not know whether it had formerly been Zhuangzi dreaming that he was a butterfly, ir it was now a butterfly dreaming that it was Zhuangzi.
> But between Zhuangzi and a butterfly there must be a difference.

--4th Century BCE Chinese philosopher Zhuangzi
