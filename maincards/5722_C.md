---
title: "Access is Bidirectional"
---

Library patrons do not only need access to materials to consume.
They need access to create, share, and disseminate.
Lankes presents this idea as the library as a platform.
Not sure about that term--it's quite loaded--but the concept is sound.
It's a democratization of the production and communication of information.

Lankes, R. *Expect More*
