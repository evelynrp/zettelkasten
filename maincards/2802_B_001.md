---
title: "Autopoiesis"
id: "2802_B_001"
---

A system is autopoietic when it produces its own elements through its own operations. Social systems reproduce themselves by generating new communications out of prior communications - the system is its own product. Borrowed from Maturana and Varela's biology, applied by Luhmann to communication rather than life.
