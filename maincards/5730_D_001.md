---
title: "Notes field as intellectual record"
id: "5730_D_001"
---

Removed or superseded metadata should be preserved in a notes field rather than discarded. The record of descriptive decisions - what language was used, what was changed and why - has archival value in its own right, for future archivists who need to understand not just what a description says but how it was produced.
