---
title: "Naming as an ethical stance"
id: "5730_A_001"
---

Cataloguing decisions - what to call something, whose terminology to use, what categories to apply - accumulate into institutional memory that persists for decades. Naming choices inherited from earlier, biased practices become "legacy data" embedded in modern records. Choosing accurate, community-informed terminology over inherited convention is not precision for its own sake; it is an ongoing ethical commitment.
