---
title: "Society = Communication"
id: "2802_A"
---

Society consists entirely of communications, not of persons, actions, or intentions. People are in the environment of social systems - not inside them. A communication occurs only when three selections are synthesized: information (what is selected), utterance (the act of saying it), and understanding (grasping that something was communicated and how). All three must occur; understanding can include rejection.
