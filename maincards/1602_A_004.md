---
title: "Semantic Externalism"
id: "1602_A_004"
---

As opposed to semantic internalism, which posits that words get their meanings from what you mentally associate with them, semantic externalism posits that meaning is the result of a causal chain.
By following the causal chains, we see that Short Napoleon and Regular-Sized Napoleon are, in fact, the same man.

Key thinkers: Ruth Barcan Marcus, Saul Kripke, Hilary Putnam
