---
title: "Structural Drift (Co-evolution)"
id: "2802_B_003_B_001"
---

When two systems remain structurally coupled over time, each evolves in response to the recurring irritations the other produces. Their internal structures gradually shape each other without either system losing operational closure. Neither system controls or causes the other's changes - each responds on its own terms, but along a shared trajectory.
