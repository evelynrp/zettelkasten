> A camera is like a gun.
> It's threatening...
> It's invasive.
> It's exploitive, in terms of mass media.

The camera, like the gaze or the spear, is penetrating, but also creates a lasting impression, a violent artifact.
In the case of 1960s Appalachia, cameras "mined the images [of poverty] the way the companies had mined the coal."
