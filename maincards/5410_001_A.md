---
title: "LaserDisc"
---

Pastic disc stamped with pits and lands, metallic reflective layer, protective lacquer.
Analog video, analog or digital audio.
Pits were closely spaced, so discs were prone to manufacturing flaws.
Favored by cinephiles, e.g. Criterion Collection, but lived and died before widespread acceptance of optical media.
Prone to disc rot, oxidation of the metal, due to imperfections in the lacquer or other manufacturing mistakes in adhesive, temperature, etc.
