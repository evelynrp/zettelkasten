---
title: "The Causal Theory of Knowledge"
id: "1602_A_003_C"
---

The causal theory of knowledge does away with justification altogether, stating that true belief need only be causally connected to the knower.

S knows that p if and only if p is causally connected in an appropriate way with S's believing p.

Key thinker: Alvin Goldman
