---
title: "Operational Closure and Cognitive Openness"
id: "2802_B_002"
---

Systems are operationally closed: they only operate through their own operations (the legal system decides legally; it cannot use economic logic directly). They are cognitively open: they can observe and react to their environment, but always through their own internal logic. Environmental events can only irritate a system - how it responds is determined by its own structure, not by the environment.
