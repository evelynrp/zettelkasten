---
title: "Functional Differentiation"
id: "2802_B_003"
---

Modern society's defining structure: society is divided into autonomous subsystems (law, economy, politics, science, religion, art, etc.), each performing a function no other system can substitute. The arrangement is heterarchical - no system dominates the others. Each is supreme within its own domain. This replaced pre-modern stratified (rank-based) differentiation and enormously increased overall social complexity.
