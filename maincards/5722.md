---
title: "Seven Steps of Community Engagement"
id: "5722"
---

1. Identifying community needs: surveys, questionaires, focus groups, etc.
2. Raising awareness: acquisitions and community discussions.
3. Promoting inclusion: season- or demographic-specific scheduling.
4. Ensuring accessibility: food, childcare, location, virtual.
5. Mediating dialogue: dispute resolution.
6. Building partnerships: local organizations.
7. Seeking external support: grans, ALA, etc.
