---
title: "Superiority of Intellect"
---

Intellect is superior to imagination, according to Descartes.
I can intellectually work out the geometry of a 1000-sided polygon, but only roughly imagine it because imagination is tied to bodily sensations.
Bodily sensations such as hunger and pain serve a survival function but must be checked against intellect.

Cartesian dualism is stupid.

Nagel, J. *Knowledge*
