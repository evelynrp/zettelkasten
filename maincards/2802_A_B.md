---
title: "Inclusion and Exclusion"
id: "2802_A_B"
---

Inclusion is how persons are made relevant addresses for a system's communications (having legal standing, being able to pay). Exclusion is being cut off from a system's communications. Individuals are never inside a system - always in its environment. Exclusion compounds: those cut off from one system tend to be cut off from others, producing cascading disadvantage.
