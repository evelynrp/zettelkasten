---
title: "Community Engagement vs. Outreach"
---

Outreach centers the library's goals while engagement centers the community's goals.
Engagement requires ongoing active partnership, and "is about giving voice to our public in the development of programs, services and more... Outreach is often focused on creating awareness and bringing services to our community outside of our library buildings."
