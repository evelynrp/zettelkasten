---
title: "Evolution"
id: "2802_D"
---

Society evolves through variation (communications that deviate from expectation), selection (some variations stabilize as structures), and retention (selected structures are preserved as memory). Evolution has no direction or goal. Functional differentiation was not inevitable - it emerged contingently as one solution to managing complexity. Semantics (the stock of available concepts and self-descriptions) evolves alongside social structure but is not reducible to it.
