---
title: "Community as an Immune System"
---

In 1967, Hobart Ison, a property owner in Eastern Kentucky, shot and murdered Canadian filmmaker Hugh O'Connor.
After decades of exploitative and insulting media coverage of Appalachian poverty, Ison's community rallied around him without waiting to hear all the facts.
Community inherently protects those within, even those with poor reputations (Ison was known as a bad landlord with a short temper), from those without.
"Ison should have just been thrown in the slammer, but this was a poem about Eastern Kentucky that was beautiful."

Barret, E. *Stranger with a Camera*
