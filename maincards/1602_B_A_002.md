---
title: "Pyrrhonian Scepticism"
id: "1602_B_A_002"
---

A more radical response to Academic Scepticism.
Pyrrhonian Sceptics avoid dogmatism by refusing to make up their minds one way or another.
"It might be and it might not be."
Schroedinger's epistemology.

Key thinkers: Pyrrho of Elis (360-270 BCE), Sextus Empiricus (160-210 CE)
