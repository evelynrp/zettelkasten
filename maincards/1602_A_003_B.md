---
title: "Gettier Cases"
---

Edmund Gettier challenged the classical analysis with some fun thought puzzles.
Here's one:
A desert traveller is searching for water.
He sees, in the valley ahead, a shimmering blue expanse.
Unfortunately, it's a mirage.
But fortunately, there actually is water, hidden under a rock.
Did the traveller know, as he stood on the hilltop hallucinating, that there was water ahead?
