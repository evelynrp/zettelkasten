---
title: "Second-Order Observation"
id: "2802_C"
---

First-order: observing what is. Second-order: observing how observers observe - what distinctions they use, what their blind spots are. Modern society is distinguished by the prevalence of second-order observation (science watches how knowledge is made; art reflects on aesthetic perception). Every observer has a blind spot: the distinction used to observe cannot be seen from within that same observation. Luhmann's own theory is a second-order observation from within science.
