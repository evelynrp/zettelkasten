---
title: "Archival are never neutral"
id: "5730_A"
---

Archival description is an act with political and historical consequences. The categories used to organize records shape what researchers can find and how they interpret it. Archivists construct the historical record - they do not passively transmit it. Claiming neutrality is itself a professional ideology that obscures real descriptive choices.
