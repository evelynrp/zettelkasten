---
title: "Philosophy of Audio Preservation"
---

Preserve content, not form.
Distribution is preservation.
The major question is, is the audio's "truest form" how its original audience heart it, how the artist produced it, or how the artist would have produced it with today's technology?
Related to Schuller's Types.
