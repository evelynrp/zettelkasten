---
title: "Defining Community"
---

The word "community" evokes "images and feelings of identity, shared circumstance, and common cause" and is "dynamic--in some sense a moving target--reshaped and needing to respond to significant macrostructural forces"; "theories are concerned with the nature and foundation of interactions among individuals within social groups, and with the function, structure, and processes through which collective identity, norms, and the possibility of action are established and institutionalized."

Chaskin, R. *Theories of Community*
