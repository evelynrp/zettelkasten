---
title: "Structural Coupling"
id: "2802_B_003_B"
---

The mechanism by which operationally closed systems affect each other without merging. Systems adjust their structures so that one system's operations can serve as irritations for another. A sales contract is simultaneously legal and economic communication - coupling law and economy without fusing them. Language structurally couples consciousness and communication. Coupling is selective and stable, not direct causation.
