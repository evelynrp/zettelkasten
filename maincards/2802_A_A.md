---
title: "Meaning"
id: "2802_A_A"
---

Meaning is the medium in which both consciousness and communication operate. Every meaningful act points beyond itself - selecting one possibility implies a horizon of others that remain available. This surplus of possibility must constantly be managed. Meaning operates across three dimensions: factual (what is vs. what else could be), temporal (now vs. past/future), and social (my perspective vs. yours).
